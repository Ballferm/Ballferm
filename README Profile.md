## 📊 Research

[**Comparative Evaluation of ML for Network Intrusion Detection**](https://github.com/Ballferm/ml-ids-comparative-evaluation) (MSc dissertation)

Do ML intrusion-detection rankings hold up on unseen data? I compared five supervised models across **NSL-KDD, UNSW-NB15 and CICIDS2017**, with cross-validation, statistical testing and per-attack-category analysis. The evaluation focuses on what matters to SOC analysts, such as false positives and alert fatigue, not just raw accuracy.
